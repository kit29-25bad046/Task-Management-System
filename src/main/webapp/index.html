PriorityQueue<Task> Execution

Active pending and in-progress tasks are processed through a Java PriorityQueue<Task> min-heap. Elements are ordered primarily by priority rank (HIGH > MEDIUM > LOW) and secondary by earliest deadline date. Completed tasks are automatically excluded from the dispatch queue.
